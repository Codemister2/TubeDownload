Client-side Terraria/tModLoader performance mod focused on smoother high-FPS rendering and scalable optimization without changing gameplay speed.

Features:
- Configurable render cap from 60-1000 FPS plus Unlimited
- Terraria gameplay simulation remains at 60 UPS
- Near-screen player, NPC, projectile, item and camera interpolation
- Auto, Balanced, Performance, LowEnd, Potato and Custom presets
- Adaptive particle/weather budgets based on measured FPS
- Dust, gore and rain limiting with off-screen cosmetic culling
- Background, distortion, storm, wave and lighting optimizations
- Low-end and potato-PC modes for much weaker hardware
- Menu and unfocused FPS limiters to reduce wasted CPU/GPU use
- Live FPS/UPS and optimization diagnostics overlay
- /terraboost runtime commands
